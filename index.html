1. What the application does

Social Media Agent is a private tool that prepares short vertical videos and uploads them to TikTok and Instagram accounts owned or managed by the operator. It connects to these platforms only through their official interfaces: the TikTok Content Posting API with TikTok Login Kit, and the Instagram Graph API with Facebook Login. It does not collect passwords and does not automate a web browser.

2. Who may use it

The application is not offered to the public. Only the operator, and people the operator has explicitly authorized, may connect accounts. By connecting an account you confirm that you own it or are authorized to manage it.

  • You follow the TikTok Terms of Service, TikTok Community Guidelines, Instagram Terms of Use and Instagram Community Guidelines.
  • You review content before it is published, where the application asks you to.

4. Platform services

TikTok and Instagram are operated by third parties. Their availability, rules and decisions, including removal of content or restrictions of accounts, are outside the operator's control. Your use of those platforms remains subject to their own terms.

5. Revoking access

You can disconnect the application at any time: in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions; and in Facebook under Settings, Business integrations. After revocation the stored access tokens no longer work and are deleted by the operator on request.

Processing is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, because it is necessary to provide the upload function you requested when connecting your account, and on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for the operator's legitimate interest in operating the application securely.

4. Storage and security

All data is stored locally on the operator's own computer in a database. Access tokens are encrypted before they are stored. Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone except the services listed below.

5. Services involved

  • TikTok (TikTok Technology Limited, Ireland) receives the uploaded videos and captions.
  • Meta (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited) receives the uploaded videos and captions for Instagram.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) receives video titles and topic categories to suggest captions and hashtags. No account data or access tokens are sent to Anthropic.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Union. They do so under their own privacy policies and the safeguards provided for in Art. 44 et seq. GDPR, such as EU standard contractual clauses.

6. Retention

Access tokens are kept as long as the account stays connected and are deleted when you revoke access or ask for deletion. Upload records are kept for up to 24 months to avoid duplicate posts and are then deleted.
